Pacific Brands — Retail & in-store

Retail digital experience

Enhancing experiences across major consumer web properties and in-store digital touchpoints — raising retail digital engagement 26% with a customer-led approach.

Timeline

2007 — 2011

Role

Digital Design Manager

Scope

Web & in-store UX, Behavioural analysis, A/B testing, IA

Reach

Major consumer brands

Challenge

Bridge the web and the shop floor into one experience.

Across major consumer brands, the digital experience lived in one world and the store in another. Customers moved fluidly between them, but the touchpoints didn't — leaving engagement and acquisition on the table.

As Digital Design Manager, I led a customer-led effort to connect web properties and in-store digital touchpoints, and to make the whole system measurable.

Approach

Follow the behaviour, then design for it.

I grounded the work in behavioural analysis and journey optimisation — understanding how people actually moved across channels — and used quantitative A/B testing to validate what worked before scaling it.

To support growth without fragility, I streamlined interaction design production and backed a scalable CMS migration with comprehensive IA deliverables, including sitemaps and page flows.

A customer-led experience, online and in-store

By designing web and in-store touchpoints around real customer needs, retail digital engagement rose 26% — a more joined-up experience that customers actually responded to.

Acquisition, driven by evidence

Through behavioural analysis, journey optimisation, and quantitative A/B testing, user acquisition across multiple online channels accelerated 17% — growth built on data, not guesswork.

A platform built to scale

I streamlined interaction design production and supported a scalable CMS migration, decreasing platform downtime 14% through comprehensive IA deliverables — sitemaps, page flows, and the structures a growing digital estate depends on.
